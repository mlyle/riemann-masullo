# What the globally normed theta restriction orders in strip width

Consider the rational principal character. Fix a Sobolev order $s\ge3$. Let $x=\log|g|$ and write
$w_c(x)=e^{2c|x|}(1+x^2)^{s/2}$ for the Hilbert-space density at half-width $c\ge0$ relative to class Haar measure. The centered theta representative of a source $f$ is $|g|^{1/2}\theta f(g)$ at every width. The test action on representatives is the same Haar convolution. These facts follow from [§9.2](https://github.com/mccorvie/riemann-masullo/blob/3ac04f89adf94c01d82bcb1c6b4f30c1d9d2535a/RiemannMasullo/Ch09_HardySobolev/Sec2.lean#L3069), [§9.3](https://github.com/mccorvie/riemann-masullo/blob/3ac04f89adf94c01d82bcb1c6b4f30c1d9d2535a/RiemannMasullo/Ch09_HardySobolev/Sec3.lean#L867) and [§10.1](https://github.com/mccorvie/riemann-masullo/blob/3ac04f89adf94c01d82bcb1c6b4f30c1d9d2535a/RiemannMasullo/Ch10_TraceSpectral/Sec1.lean#L624).

## The two comparisons

At a fixed edge $b$, [#7514](https://github.com/mccorvie/riemann-masullo/pull/7514) asks for $D_{b-}\le D_b$. Each real signed trace has a minus sign in front of the zero sum. An occupied edge with positive test gives $D_{b-}-D_b>0$, with the Sobolev boundary multiplicity. Separately, for the one test per character in [#7556](https://github.com/mccorvie/riemann-masullo/pull/7556), the target is $\lim_{c\downarrow0}D_c\le D_{1/2}$. Given the specified projected tensors, the left side minus the right side equals the **full** positive mass off the central line. This is a comparison between separated widths. The positive-width limit need not be the spectral trace evaluated using a cap at width zero.

Penner's [ordered physical stage theorem](https://github.com/sergmister/riemann-masullo/blob/394da1b3caac72bb160db3ee4007b320426a7556/RiemannMasullo/Research/TraceComparison/PhysicalThetaBands.lean#L212) gives at each fixed width the bandwidth limit of the geometric trace at a fixed physical cutoff, then its limit as the physical cutoff is exhausted. The final sign is $-\mathrm{Tr}(A_\varphi P_{\rm spec})$. The admissibility $\varphi(1)=0$ gives zero full localized trace. This gives the same signed value as the zero sum. Identifying it with #7556's particular smoothed diagonal uses that PR's representing-tensor hypothesis.

## Covariance on a common observation window

For a bounded padded window $W$, let $J_c$ restrict the globally normed theta space $\Theta_c$ into the fixed $L^2(W,d\mu)$ using Haar measure, and put $K_c=J_c J_c^*$. For $0\le a\le b$, the inclusion $I_{ba}:\Theta_b\longrightarrow\Theta_a$ is contractive. It is the identity on common theta representatives and extends from their dense span by the pointwise weight inequality. Hence $J_b=J_a I_{ba}$ and $0\le K_b\le K_a$. The same argument works with the common set of generators of a fixed Fourier band. If $T=M_\rho A_\varphi$ has input and output in $W$, the globally normed geometric trace is the trace on Haar window space of $M_{w_c}T K_c$. This is the weighted-coordinate version of [Penner's restriction identity](https://github.com/sergmister/riemann-masullo/blob/63ea7fc880fdf27febeea6f5acdef268c506b7b7/RiemannMasullo/Research/TraceComparison/WindowRestriction.lean#L73). The scalar $w_c$ in this pairing cannot be discarded.

At a fixed $b>0$, $K_a$ decreases strongly to $K_{b-}$ as $a\uparrow b$. In fact $K_{b-}$ is the covariance of $\Theta_b^-=\{u\in H_b:u\in\Theta_a\text{ for every }0<a<b\}$ with its $H_b$ norm. To see this, apply the dual norm formula to $\|J_a^*h\|$, along a countable cofinal sequence $a_j\uparrow b$. Select near-maximizers in the closed unit ball, with the phase of the scalar pairing fixed. For each $j$ the later vectors lie in the weakly compact unit ball of $\Theta_{a_j}$. A diagonal weakly convergent subsequence has compatible limits there because the injections are continuous. The common representative $u$ has $\int|u|^2 w_b\le1$ by monotone convergence. Its window pairing attains the limiting dual norm. Conversely every such $u$ belongs to every narrower unit ball. Polarization and monotone convergence of bounded positive operators give the identification. Thus $K_{b-}-K_b$ is the observation of the positive orthogonal projection onto $\Theta_b^-\ominus\Theta_b$ in $H_b$. The width-intersection theorem of #7514 identifies the missing annihilation conditions as edge jets. For trace-class raw $T$ on the padded window the trace pairing with $K_a$ converges by strong convergence and boundedness, since $w_a\to w_b$ uniformly on $W$. The resulting open-minus-closed sign is the sign of the pairing with **this positive defect**. Positivity of the real part of $M_{w_b}T$ would give open $\ge$ closed. The wanted inequality would follow instead from a nonpositive pairing on the defect. For #7556 the two endpoint weights do not coincide, so even this simplification at a single edge cannot be used for the separated-width comparison.

## Both signs on genuine rational one-vector compressions

The following concerns rank-one subspaces inside a common rational finite band, at one fixed sufficiently large admissible physical cutoff. The probe can be chosen among those of #7556. The rational principal divisor is invariant under $w\mapsto-w$ and conjugation in the centered coordinate. Replace its radial $H$ by $(\Re H(x)+\Re H(-x))/2$. The real Mellin value is the average of the original four real values on that orbit. Both polar zeros, $H(0)=0$ and positivity at every actual zero survive. The resulting smooth compact real even $H$ is nonzero. Choose $t\ne0$ with $H(t)\ne0$, and a radial physical cutoff $\rho$ whose plateau contains points $x_1,x_2$ with $x_2-x_1=t$ and small neighborhoods of them. Fix a bounded window $W$ containing the output support of $\rho$ and its sum with the negative support of $H$.

For smooth nonnegative bumps of integral one around $x_1,x_2$ of common width $\eta$, let $f_\pm$ be their sum or difference. The numerator $n_\pm(c)$ of the real compressed trace on any vector agreeing with $f_\pm$ in $W$ is
$$
n_\pm(c)=v^2\iint \rho(x)w_c(x) H(x-y) f_\pm(y)f_\pm(x)\,dy\,dx,
$$
where $v>0$ is the mass of the norm-one idèle classes; the squared norm in the denominator uses $v\,dx$. The two diagonal terms tend to zero, and the cross terms tend to $\pm v^2 H(t)[w_c(x_1)+w_c(x_2)]$, uniformly for $0\le c\le1/2$. This is uniform continuity on a compact set. Fix $\eta$ small enough that the two numerators are bounded away from zero with opposite signs. Their width derivatives insert $2|x|$ and are bounded uniformly. Consequently $A=\max_{\pm,c}|n_\pm'(c)/n_\pm(c)|$ is finite.

Choose genuine polar-free rational band-limited theta inputs $u_\pm$ agreeing with $f_\pm$ on $W$, using [the explicit interpolation theorem](https://github.com/sergmister/riemann-masullo/blob/63ea7fc880fdf27febeea6f5acdef268c506b7b7/RiemannMasullo/Research/TraceComparison/RationalWindow.lean#L25). In its reciprocal variable $r=e^{-x}$ the arithmetically normalized theta is $2v r g(r)$, and the centered representative has the additional factor $r^{-1/2}$. For the unaveraged radial theta, divide by $v$. Thus the required $g$ on the reciprocal window is the prescribed smooth profile times a fixed smooth nonvanishing factor. Extend evenly and smoothly by zero off a positive compact interval. Enlarging the bandwidth accommodates the support.

Next choose $K>A+1$ so large that $W\subset[-K,K]$. The very same interpolation theorem supplies a **nonzero hidden vector** $v_0$ vanishing on all of $[-K,K]$. Use reciprocal threshold $a=e^{-K}$, a nonzero even reciprocal bump supported for $|r|\in(e^{K+1},e^{K+2})$, and bandwidth larger than $e^{K+2}$. The formula applies at every $x\le K$. It vanishes on $[-K,K]$ and is nonzero near $-K-1$. Any uncontrolled tail also satisfies $|x|>K$. All three raw vectors belong to a common finite additive band upon increasing it once. Their standard finite factors are unramified. They belong to every width by the global theta embedding.

Put $u_{\pm,\lambda}=u_\pm+\lambda v_0$ and $d_{\pm,\lambda}(c)=\|u_{\pm,\lambda}\|_{H_c}^2$. Localization leaves $n_\pm(c)$ unchanged. The ratio $d'_{\pm,\lambda}/d_{\pm,\lambda}$ converges uniformly on $[0,1/2]$ as $\lambda\to\infty$ to
$$
\frac{\int 2|x| |v_0|^2w_c\,d\mu}{\int |v_0|^2w_c\,d\mu}\ge2K.
$$
Indeed, after division by $\lambda^2$ both quantities are quadratic polynomials in $1/\lambda$ with uniformly bounded coefficients, and the limiting denominator is bounded below by $\|v_0\|_{H_0}^2>0$. To differentiate and bound coefficients, take any slightly wider strip, which dominates $|x|w_c$ on this compact range of widths.

The actual rank-one trace is $q_\pm(c)=n_\pm(c)/d_{\pm,\lambda}(c)$. For sufficiently large fixed $\lambda$, $q'_\pm/q_\pm=n'_\pm/n_\pm-d'_{\pm,\lambda}/d_{\pm,\lambda}<0$ throughout the width interval. The positive trace strictly decreases; the negative trace strictly increases. This disproves a hereditary comparison $q_c\le q_{1/2}$ over all genuine theta subspaces even for the positive-at-every-zero probe and all small $c>0$. Each of these finite-rank traces remains continuous from below at every fixed $b$. More generally, for fixed independent common theta generators, continuity follows from $\mathrm{tr}(G_c^{-1}H_c)$ for their positive definite Gram matrix and the matrix of the localized action.

Penner's full bandwidth stage can be infinite dimensional even at a fixed bandwidth. This rank-one calculation does not determine its trace, the exhaustion in physical cutoff, or the aggregate pairing of the actual boundary defect. It locates an explicit failure of a sign bound on arbitrary genuine subcompressions. The signed comparison of the complete projection in #5571 is open.
